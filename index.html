            Your Privacy Matters

            Welcome to VMPix!

            VMPix is a personal photography project operated as Voodoo Media from Brunswick, Maine, United States. Voodoo Media/VMPix is responsible for the analytics choices and use of information described here. You can contact the operator at ruyven12@gmail.com.

            We would like to use Google Analytics to better understand how visitors explore our website, from music and wrestling photography to galleries and individual photos. This helps us learn what people enjoy, which parts of the archive receive attention, and how we can make the experience better.

            Optional Google Analytics is available only after you accept it. Until you give a valid acceptance, Google Analytics does not load or collect information through this website. The sections below explain how this consent-first setup works.

            We do not use Google Analytics for personalized advertising, and we do not use it to intentionally identify individual visitors. You can use VMPix whether you accept or reject analytics.

            What We Collect

            Once analytics is enabled, and only after you accept it, our current implementation records page views for public pages you visit. Google Analytics may also automatically record technical session and engagement information, including a first visit, the start of a session, and time spent engaging with the site. These automatic measurements are separate from custom tracking of website features.

            Analytics may provide approximate geographic information, browser and device details, referral sources, and approved campaign-source labels. Google receives your IP address as part of the connection and uses it to derive approximate location. This is not precise location tracking, but it does mean Google receives information about your device and visit.

            We do not intentionally include names, email addresses, free-form text, or other directly identifying information in our analytics event payloads. That does not make all analytics information anonymous: device and browser identifiers and related technical information can still be personal information.

            After acceptance, V3 measures page visits, confirmed gallery opens, active photo views and clicks on the existing Wrestling photo-download control. These measurements may include the archive category, available public gallery or photo identifiers, photo position and the type of viewing action. A download-button click records a request to download, not proof that an image was successfully saved. After acceptance, V3 also measures settled archive search usage and loaded result counts without search terms, along with filter interactions using category labels and selection counts without the selected values. We do not send search text, names or query-derived identifiers through these events. Google's technical session and engagement measurements also apply after acceptance. V3 also measures navigation through existing links between related archive records and the opening of supported information panels. These measurements include archive and content categories, panel types and original public record identifiers when available. Closing panels, automatic restoration and passive rendering do not create these interaction events. Sharing and other unconnected capabilities remain outside this custom tracking. We will update this notice before connecting tracking that changes what we collect or why we collect it.

            Cookies & Your Choices

            Our worldwide setup uses Basic Consent Mode. Before you give a valid acceptance, we do not load Google Analytics or send Analytics requests. Rejecting analytics, withdrawing acceptance, or allowing acceptance to expire prevents new website analytics events. Advertising storage, advertising user data, Google Signals, and personalized advertising remain disabled even if you accept analytics.

            After acceptance, Google Analytics may use analytics cookies and related identifiers to measure visits. You can reject analytics, change your choice, or withdraw acceptance at any time using the Analytics preferences button on this page or the website's privacy controls.

            Events recorded while your acceptance was valid may remain buffered in Google's software and may be transmitted after you withdraw or reject analytics. We cannot guarantee cancellation of previously queued or in-progress transmissions. This technical limitation does not mean that every later transmission is lawful; the privacy rules and visitor rights that apply still need to be respected.

            When local storage is available, we save your choice on your device so that we can remember and respect it. This preference storage is separate from optional analytics. If storage is unavailable, we may be unable to remember your choice on a later visit, and Analytics stays off when we cannot verify permission. Where a data-protection legal basis is required for remembering your choice, we rely on our legitimate interest in respecting your privacy preferences.

            How Information Is Used

            When enabled with your acceptance, analytics helps us understand which parts of VMPix are useful, improve navigation, and make the archive easier to explore. We rely on your consent for this optional analytics processing. Accepting analytics is not required to browse the website.

            Google is the recipient of analytics information and provides the Google Analytics service as a processor under its applicable data processing terms. Google's own privacy information also explains how it handles information received through sites using its services. Information may be processed internationally, including in the United States.

            We have reviewed Google's published data processing and international-transfer terms. Where relevant, this may involve verified coverage under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or its UK Extension, or applicable Standard Contractual Clauses and any additional required safeguards. We are not claiming that a particular transfer mechanism has already been verified for this setup.

            You can read how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google's Privacy Policy, and Google's data processing terms.

            Data Retention

            Our Google Analytics event and user-data retention setting is 14 months, with Reset on new activity switched off. New activity therefore does not restart the retention period for a user identifier. This setting does not mean that all analytics records or aggregate reports are automatically or immediately deleted 14 months after a visit; Google's deletion processing and separate treatment of aggregate reports still apply.

            We use Google's default analytics-cookie expiration and renewal settings, with no custom overrides. Google documents a two-year default for its _ga and _ga_<container-id> cookies, with expiration renewed relative to the most recent visit while analytics is permitted. Browsers may impose shorter limits or block cookies altogether. Cookie lifetime is separate from the 14-month event-data retention setting and the 180-day preference period described here.

            A saved analytics preference is valid for up to 180 days. An expired preference is removed when the site next runs and can access local storage, and a fresh choice is needed before optional analytics can resume. Local storage does not delete itself when that period ends: if you do not return to the site, or storage cannot be accessed, an expired record may remain on your device longer. You can also remove it using your browser's site-data controls.

            Rejecting analytics, withdrawing acceptance, or allowing acceptance to expire prevents new website analytics events. These actions do not instantly erase information already collected. We assess and carry out any deletion required by applicable law, according to the information we can identify and the rights that apply. You can also contact us about a deletion request.

            Your Privacy Rights

            You can change or withdraw your analytics choice whenever you like through the website's privacy controls. Depending on the law that applies to your information and the basis for processing it, you may also have rights to access a copy, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, restrict processing, object to processing, or receive certain information in a portable form.

            Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Where applicable, you may also complain to the relevant data-protection authority. The availability of these rights depends on your circumstances; operating this personal project in Maine does not mean every visitor is covered by the same privacy laws.

            For privacy questions or requests, contact ruyven12@gmail.com. We may need enough information to verify and respond to your request.

                                      Known Safeguards & Limitations
                                      • Per-collection locking does not guarantee global exclusivity.
                                      • Manual database edits are not automatically protected from existing imports.
                                      • Imports started outside Admin can operate independently.
                                      • Lock availability cannot be assumed when status reporting fails.
                                      • Existing lock acquisition can be bypassed after a storage error; locking is not guaranteed in that case.

                                      This dashboard reports known limitations. It does not create or change import protection.
