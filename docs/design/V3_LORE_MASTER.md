## 2. V3 Archive Core Lore

**CANON — The Archive (WB, Global Lore).** Older than any individual world, neither city nor planet, the Archive connects worlds. No one remembers who first created it. It preserves memories, relationships, history, creativity, discovery and legacy. “Every world exists because the Archive remembers.” Do not invent an origin to resolve this ambiguity.

**CANON — Archive Principle (WB).** The Archive preserves communities rather than celebrating fame: people, relationships, creativity, competition, discovery, history and emotion. Worlds are narrative places, not UI themes. Identity precedes visuals and story precedes implementation.

**CANON — Archive Energy (WB).** Ancient energy is both technology and magic. Worlds express it through runes, glyphs, living energy, sacred geometry, Voodoo symbols, arcane machinery, light and motion. No source equates Draegin with Archive Energy or establishes a shared origin.

**APPROVED / PROTECTED — Console/Core (WB Portfolio lock and system descriptions).** The Engine HUD is the Archive Console, communicating Archive state and destination tuning. It presents Current View, Archive Core, Archive Seal, Archive Build and System State. The Core is its heart: alive, ancient, restrained and subtly mechanical. Information is projected from the Engine, not the stars. The Console stays fixed while the observatory moves.

**APPROVED / PROTECTED — Lifeblood, scan and analysis (WB).** These identities belong to the Portfolio lock. Selected world color tunes the energy. The scan locates, reads and prepares the destination; Archive Analysis reports title, description and Archive Status.

**DEFERRED — Detailed circulation (WB, Lifeblood System).** Core → Engine circulation → Archive Seal → Archive Build remains explicitly described as a future concept in its source section. Preserve that qualification alongside the locked Lifeblood identity. Mixed future/current source snapshots do not prove every effect is implemented.

**APPROVED / PROTECTED — Interface identity (EB).** `ARCHIVE BUILD 3.0.01` is V3 interface lore, not an inferred in-world date or historical chronology.

The Four Pillars are Voice, Guitar, Bass and Drums; travel is represented by sound waves. Law: “Everyone belongs.” Music unites; exclusion, ego and division do not define this world.

## 5. Daïion / The Battleground

### Identity and deliberate ambiguity

**APPROVED / PROTECTED — Identity (PD W1; P29).** The Battleground is the Wrestling gateway/world designation within the larger Archive. Daïion is the planet/world itself, not merely a UI theme. `PLANET // DAÏION` identifies the gateway. Hall of Crusades, Hall of Champions and Fields of Conflict are destinations within Daïion, not replacement names for the whole world.

**WORKING LORE — Exact nature (P29).** Preserve intentional ambiguity: literal world, Archive reconstruction, or something between. Do not choose an answer or invent a reconstruction mechanism.

### Existing Battleground canon

**CANON — Hall of Legacy (WB, World: The Battleground).** The Battleground is an ancient Hall of Legacy built around a timeless colosseum. The Archive did not create this place; it discovered it. Ancient stone, weathered steel, sacred ceremonial architecture, runes and Archive/Voodoo energy preserve and enhance original structures rather than replacing them.

**CANON — Purpose and inhabitants (WB).** Competition creates stories; stories become legacy; legacy earns respect. “Victory fades. Legacy remains.” This world preserves honorable competition, not violence for its own sake. Performers, fans, referees, ring/production crews, announcers, promoters, photographers, commentators, contributors, veterans and newcomers all contribute.

**CANON — Atmosphere and growth (WB).** Constant storms, lightning, rain-darkened walls, eternal flames and drifting embers make the place timeless rather than hostile. History deepens with each generation; the world grows through accumulated legacy rather than physical size. Future arenas, halls, archives and districts are discoveries within an ancient world.

**CANON — Landmark, travel and echoes (WB).** The Eternal Championship is an immense belt forged by the Archive, belonging to no individual and representing every competitor: legacy rather than victory. Ceremonial Archive Portals connect halls, arenas, archives and districts. Significant moments leave impressions on the arena; walls echo with battles of yesteryear through atmosphere, sound or emotion. Law: “Every battle leaves a legacy.” These definitions do not authorize deferred Portfolio transport or establish new powers.

**UNRESOLVED — Planet/Hall scope (WB versus P29).** Preserve the Hall/colosseum canon and supplied planetary history below. Their geographic, historical and metaphysical relationship is unspecified. Do not make the Hall a capital, survivor city, reconstruction or later era by inference. War-torn history does not replace the canonical honor/respect identity.

### Daïion Before the Battleground

**WORKING LORE — Supplied history (P29).** Daïion was once prosperous, rich with life and considered a beacon of its star system. Advanced civilizations and enormous mega-cities existed there. Technological prosperity was tied heavily to Draegin. It was not always war-torn; generations of exploitation and conflict transformed it.

**UNRESOLVED — Decline (P29).** Detailed causes and sequence remain unfinished. No dates, named civilizations, factions, wars, perpetrators, environmental mechanisms or endpoint chronology are supplied. Do not claim Draegin alone caused the transformation.
