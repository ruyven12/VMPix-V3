## Working story anchors

**WORKING LORE.** ZHENTO is the **PLANET OF CULTURE**. **MUSIC IS THE SOUNDTRACK OF LIFE**: life, memory, identity, survival, connection, culture, movement and emotion.

Music is the world's heartbeat: **THE PULSE**. Rhythm and drums communicate pulse; bass, drums, guitar tones and other frequencies are heard and physically felt. The Pulse is not a magical resource, fuel system or equivalent of Draegin. Its physical/metaphysical explanation remains open.

Zhento's prosperous past was an enormous, highly developed industrial mega-city civilization with music and culture foundational to everyday life. Its present is largely deserted, abandoned and decayed. No cause of decline or fate of the population is established.

The visitor is **THE LISTENER**, following and interpreting what remains rather than becoming a hero or conqueror. The Pulse guides the journey.

## Emotional and sonic direction

**DESIGN DIRECTION.** **WELCOME → RESONANCE → ECHO → EMPTINESS**. The Pulse draws the Listener in; warmth from music coexists with loneliness in deserted spaces. Feel the Pulse before explaining it.

Blue/purple sonic waves, waveforms, rhythm patterns, frequency-like motion, branching signals, convergence paths, resonance, pulses and nodes/nexuses express sound. This is narrative/visual vocabulary, not an effects plan or a change to the protected purple Shell accent. Do not recolor Daïion effects.

Rock, metal, rap, hip-hop, techno/electronic, folk, yacht rock and other archived genres belong. Regional rhythms may vary; fixed genre districts, boundaries and distribution are unresolved.

## Destination boundaries

**WORKING LORE / DESIGN DIRECTION.** Bands = sources; Shows = convergence nexuses; Venues = vessels; People = **The Chorus** (working canon / provisional).

Bands, Shows and Venues have no finalized in-world area names. Shows are convergence events; Venues are their physical containers, with varied size and architecture. Fields of Conflict is at most a structural comparison, not a design to duplicate. People are not a Hall of Fame.

**DEFERRED.** Chapter One prose, exact arrival staging, animation/effects, timing, routes, layouts, assets, audio systems and technical mechanisms await separate work. No requirement to autoplay sound or provide a physical sensation mechanism is established.
